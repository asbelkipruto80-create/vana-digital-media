Common questions

Do you work with clients outside Kenya?

Yes. I design for Kenyan listings and for agents, landlords and developers abroad, including diaspora sellers. Content is in English, with Swahili captions on request.

What about time zones?

I’m based in Kenya (EAT, UTC+3). Delivery is counted in days, not hours, so time zones rarely slow anything down. Message any time and I’ll reply during my working day.

What do I need to send?

Photos, price, location, contact details, and your logo and brand colours if you have them.

Can I request changes?

Each batch includes one revision round. Extra rounds can be added if you need them.

Do I own the designs?

Yes. Once payment is complete, the files are yours to use on any platform.
